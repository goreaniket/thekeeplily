How to manage your store

Follow these simple steps. You do not need technical knowledge.

1. Products

Open Products → Add product. Add name, price, photos, stock and description. Press Save product.

2. Orders

Open Orders to see customer purchases, payment status and order details. Check new orders regularly.

3. Shipments

After packing an order, open Shipments. Enter courier name, tracking number and tracking link.

4. Custom requests

Open Custom requests to read personalised gift requests and update their progress.

5. Categories

Use Categories to add or organise groups such as Candles, Cards and Gift Sets.

6. Website content

Use Website content to manage featured products, category visibility and homepage settings.

7. Customers & users

Customers shows shoppers. Users & roles is for managing staff access. Change roles only when needed.

8. Billing & payments

Check payment records and print invoices for paid orders. Never mark a payment paid manually.

Daily routine: Check Orders → prepare and update Shipments → reply to Custom requests. Add or update Products whenever needed.
